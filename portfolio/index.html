Independent Developer & Maker • Japan

Hello, I’m Najmul — I build useful things for the web, Android, AI & a little Web3.

I’m a student and independent developer based in Japan. DevNazu is my home on the internet — where small, everyday ideas become real products. My tagline says it best: “Small ideas. Useful things. Built with curiosity.”

I started by asking simple questions — why is this task hard, why isn’t there a simpler tool — and learned to answer them with code. Today that means React & TypeScript websites, an Android learning app on Google Play (Nihongo Path Books), AI-powered small tools like Receipt AI for life in Japan, and Web3 experiments like NAZU Token on Sepolia testnet.

  • Approach — Build. Learn. Improve. Ship something real, learn from it, make it better.
  • Focus — Useful digital products people actually need every day, not demos that go nowhere.
  • Toolkit summary — React, TypeScript, Tailwind, Supabase, Git & GitHub, Android prototyping, AI workflows, Ethereum / ERC-20.
  • Currently exploring — Smarter AI tools for everyday life in Japan and deeper Web3 building.
