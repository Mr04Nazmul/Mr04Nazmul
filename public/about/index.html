Who is Hossain Najmul?

Software Developer & Creator of DevNazu

Hossain Najmul is an independent software and Android developer based in Japan. He runs DevNazu and created Nihongo Path Books, an Android app for learning Japanese with Bengali explanations.

His projects include Japanese exam practice, accessible web interfaces and tools that connect apps to data and authentication services.
