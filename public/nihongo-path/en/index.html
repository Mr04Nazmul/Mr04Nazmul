Your Japanese study path, with Bangla support.

Nihongo Path is a Japanese learning app for Bengali speakers. On Google Play, it is listed as Nihongo Path Books.

Learn Japanese in Bangla with Nihongo Path for Android. Explore JLPT N5–N1 vocabulary, grammar, kanji stroke animation, pronunciation and writing practice.
