Nihongo Path

Nihongo Path is a Japanese learning app for Bengali speakers. On Google Play, it is listed as Nihongo Path Books.

Learn Japanese in Bangla with Nihongo Path for Android. Explore JLPT N5–N1 vocabulary, grammar, kanji stroke animation, pronunciation and writing practice.

• Vocabulary, grammar and kanji organised from N5 to N1
• Bengali meanings and grammar explanations
• Japanese pronunciation playback
• Animated kanji stroke order
• Writing-board practice and favourite words for review

Free previews are available. Additional study access uses rewarded-ad credits and time-limited access. Ad availability varies by device, region and network. An account and internet connection may be needed for full study access.

An independent learning app, not affiliated with or endorsed by the JLPT organisers. No exam result or passing outcome is guaranteed.
